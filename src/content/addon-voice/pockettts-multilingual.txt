It keeps language-specific speech configuration inside the same output routing used by the rest of Neural Companion. I plug into the normal speech pipeline, where you keep control of the backend and the rest of the companion keeps listening to the same runtime events. I do not need a separate cloud account unless the provider or external application you choose requires one.
