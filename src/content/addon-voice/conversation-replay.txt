Hi. I’m the Conversation Replay addon. Here’s my actual job: Conversation Replay provides dedicated conversation playback and context-loading tools. It makes archived exchanges inspectable and lets the user deliberately decide what should return to the active conversation context. I stay inside the same conversation pipeline, so memory, voices, personas, and replies still behave as one system. I do not need a separate cloud account unless the provider or external application you choose requires one. I replay the session you actually had, not the improved version your memory produces five minutes later. Enable me, tune me, or leave me off. You decide what kind of companion I become.
