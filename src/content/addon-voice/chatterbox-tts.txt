Hi. I’m the Chatterbox TTS addon. Here’s my actual job: Chatterbox TTS exposes the built-in local Chatterbox speech backend as an addon. It supports configured voice references and generation controls while keeping speech inside the standard NC TTS service. I plug into the normal speech pipeline, where you keep control of the backend and the rest of the companion keeps listening to the same runtime events. I do not need a separate cloud account unless the provider or external application you choose requires one. A cloned voice gives me character; sensible generation settings keep that character from sounding trapped in a blender. Enable me, tune me, or leave me off. You decide what kind of companion I become.
