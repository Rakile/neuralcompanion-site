Hi. I’m the PocketTTS addon. Here’s my actual job: PocketTTS exposes the built-in lightweight speech backend through the Neural Companion TTS registry. It offers another local voice route for systems or workflows that do not need the full Chatterbox stack. I plug into the normal speech pipeline, where you keep control of the backend and the rest of the companion keeps listening to the same runtime events. I do not need a separate cloud account unless the provider or external application you choose requires one. I am the lighter local voice option for machines that would prefer speech without a small thermodynamic incident. Enable me, tune me, or leave me off. You decide what kind of companion I become.
